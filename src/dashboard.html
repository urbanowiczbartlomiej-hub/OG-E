Galaxy Observations

Per-system summary of positions you have navigated past in the in-game galaxy view. Data was recorded only for galaxy pages you yourself opened. Filtered by the target positions list from settings (only those positions count toward the stats below).

Free Positions — longest confirmed empty runs

For each scanned galaxy, the longest contiguous run of systems whose chosen slot is confirmed empty. An unscanned system, or a scanned system with a different status, breaks the run — reported lengths are a lower bound on what actually exists, so they only grow as you keep scanning.

Reminders

Trasy Transportowe

Każda trasa wysyła mikroflotę z jednego lub więcej źródeł (planet i/lub księżyców) do uporządkowanej listy celów. Stojąc na dowolnym źródle, górna strefa przycisku Transport rozsyła flotę do celów po kolei. Wybieraj ze swoich planet i księżyców — nic nie trzeba wpisywać. Cel zbierania ustawia się w grze długim przytrzymaniem środkowej strefy przycisku Transport.

Advanced — text import / export (DSL)

One route per line: <src>, … = <ship>x<count> -> <target>, … — ship is an alias (MT/DT/PIO) or raw id; count takes a k suffix (×1000); a trailing m marks a moon (on either side). Editing here and pressing Apply replaces the cards above. This text is what syncs across devices.
