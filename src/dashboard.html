You set each reminder for a time you already know from your own action — the return time the game showed you when you sent the fleet, just like a phone reminder — and it rings at that time; OG-E never watches the game for events you couldn't predict. Is this private? How your topic is protected

OG-E derives this topic from your ntfy access token (SHA-256, 88 random bits). Nobody can guess it — that's roughly 3×10²⁶ possibilities — and ntfy.sh does not let anyone list or browse topics, so it can only be found if you reveal it. Treat it like a password: it is a secret URL, and anyone who learns it can read your fleet reminders and inject fake ones. Never share or post it.

Worried someone got hold of it? Change your ntfy access token above. That instantly derives a brand-new topic — the old one goes silent on its own, no other cleanup needed.

Want absolute certainty that only you can read it? Lock the topic to your account so even someone who knows its name is shut out:

  1. On ntfy.sh (web), reserve this exact topic and set its access to “deny everyone else” (only you).
  2. On your phone, delete the current subscription to this topic, then add it again.
  3. This time, sign in to the ntfy app with your ntfy username and password.

After that the topic is access-controlled: only your logged-in account can read or publish to it. (Reserving a topic is a paid ntfy.sh feature, or free on your own self-hosted ntfy server.)
